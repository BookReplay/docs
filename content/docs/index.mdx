---

BookReplay is a self-hosted Kindle highlights app. Import `My Clippings.txt`, browse your highlights by book, and review them on a spaced schedule so they stay with you.

- **Import** your Kindle's `My Clippings.txt`. Importing again adds only new highlights.
- **Browse** highlights by book, with covers and details matched from Open Library and, optionally, Google Books.
- **Review** highlights on a spaced schedule.
- **Build a daily habit.** Review five highlights a day and track your progress and streak.
- **Own your data.** Each instance runs on your server with one owner account.
